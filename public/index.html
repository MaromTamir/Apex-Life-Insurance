💡 Life Insurance Guidance for Clients

📅Buy young, pay less. Premiums are lowest in your 20s–30s. A 30-year-old pays up to 50% less than a 45-year-old for the same coverage.
🔢Coverage rule of thumb: Aim for 10–12× your annual income. Factor in debts, dependants, and mortgage balance.
📄Review annually. Major life events — marriage, new child, home purchase — are all triggers to reassess your policy level.
🏥Pair with critical illness. Life insurance covers death; critical illness covers surviving a major diagnosis. Together they close the gap.
💼Check employer cover first. Many employers offer group life insurance. Know what you already have before topping up.
📝Nominate beneficiaries. Always keep beneficiary details up to date — outdated nominations cause costly delays at claims time.
